The Uncertainty Engine is an AI workflow platform built by [digiLab](https://digilab.ai) for organisations that cannot afford to treat AI as a black box. Instead of returning bare predictions, every computation on the platform produces **quantified confidence** alongside its output — so your team always knows how much to trust a result. Whether you are building a risk model for critical infrastructure, a sensor-fusion pipeline for an autonomous system, or a decision-support tool for environmental management, the Uncertainty Engine gives you the rigour of uncertainty quantification (UQ) without requiring a dedicated research team to implement it.

<Note>
  The Uncertainty Engine is currently in **beta**. Interfaces and APIs may change between releases. Pin your SDK version in production and check the changelog before upgrading.
</Note>

## Who it's for

The platform is designed for **data scientists and engineers** working in safety-critical industries where the cost of a wrong answer — or an overconfident one — is high. Typical users include:

- **Energy and utilities** teams modelling asset degradation, grid stability, or demand forecasting under uncertain conditions
- **Infrastructure and civil engineering** organisations that need to certify models against sparse sensor data
- **Defence and aerospace** engineers building decision-support tools that must explain their reasoning under audit
- **Environmental science** teams working with incomplete or noisy observational datasets

If your domain demands answers you can defend — not just answers that score well on a leaderboard — the Uncertainty Engine was built for you.

## Key value propositions

**Transparent, explainable AI** — every node in the platform exposes typed inputs and outputs with human-readable metadata. Workflows are visual and auditable; there are no hidden layers between your data and your results.

**Works with sparse and incomplete data** — the platform's UQ-native architecture is designed from the ground up for the data-poor regimes common in engineering and science. You don't need millions of training examples to produce reliable confidence estimates.

**Quantified confidence on every output** — rather than returning a single point prediction, the platform propagates uncertainty through your entire workflow. The result is a distribution, not a number, so downstream decisions can be risk-weighted appropriately.

## How you interact with it

You can build and run AI workflows in two complementary ways:

- **Web UI** — a visual, node-based editor that lets you drag, connect, and configure nodes without writing code. Ideal for exploration, stakeholder demonstrations, and workflows that non-programmers need to inspect.
- **Python SDK** — the `uncertainty-engine` package gives programmatic access to every platform capability, from authenticating and running individual nodes to composing multi-step graphs and managing project resources.

## VisionEngine

The Uncertainty Engine family includes **VisionEngine**, a sub-product that applies the same UQ-native approach to computer vision tasks. VisionEngine nodes appear in the standard node library and can be composed with any other node type in your workflows.
